# Grok Town

The square beyond the hedge, north of the parish gate. Grove Park stays unbuilt. Havenbrook and Moonwolf names are not used here.

Press `C` to look at the square. `C` again, or Esc, comes back to the garden. A map in the top-right follows the camera. The visit is stored on the garden save as `town_seen`. The save version stays at 3.

What is there:

- Parish Gate, turned to face the hedge road.
- Town Hall, with two planters by the door.
- Market Row, stalls set against the hall's west gable.
- Reading Room, Tea Porch, Draft Loft, and Lane Post. Lane Post has the red door.
- The Reading Room and Tea Porch steps are cut to the door: each tread is the door width plus a small margin, not the wide stair that used to sit in front of them.
- A court of 94 stand-in pieces: 36 path tiles, 12 beds, 46 recoloured flowers. They are not a Kenney import.

Layout lives in `data/town.json`. The meshes are built by `scripts/town/grok_town.gd`.
