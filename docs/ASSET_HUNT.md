# Open-asset hunt

Status: not imported. Wave 1 is procedural so the garden has one palette. The next hunt is for volume, then art direction, then import.

## Accept first

CC0, MIT, BSD, Apache. Record every file in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` before it is referenced by a scene.

## Look here, in this order

1. Kenney (CC0) for stalls, fences, crates, paths.
2. Quaternius (CC0) for trees and rocks, then recolour.
3. Poly Haven and ambientCG (CC0) for ground and bark textures.
4. Godot Asset Library entries whose licence is MIT or CC0.
5. OpenGameArt and Freesound only when the file’s licence is GREEN.

## Do not take

- Anything that depicts a character from Viva Piñata, Dragon Quest, Animal Crossing, or The Sims.
- GPL art or code, until a written decision says otherwise.
- “Free” packs with no licence text.

## Art direction before import

Scale to the garden cell (about 0.92 by 0.86). Replace materials with the hedge, soil, and timber colours already in `scripts/world/dressing.gd`. No asset-store collage.
